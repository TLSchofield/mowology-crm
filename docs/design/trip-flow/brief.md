# Brief (filled)
**Name:** trip-flow   **Round:** 1
**What is being designed:** The whole driver trip flow on the crew Android phone, as three single-purpose moments plus their states: (1) "Are you driving today?" at clock-in, (2) the vehicle check (pre-trip), (3) "Finish your trip" when clocking out (post-trip). It replaces one confusing multi-purpose form (a "Report type" radio, a full-screen gate, jargon like "Queue report" / "Trip state: open"). Read `docs/plans/2026-11-24-android-trip-clock-audit.md` for why.
**Who and where:** A landscaping crew driver, outdoors in bright sun, possibly gloved, one-handed, often with weak signal, at the start and end of a shift standing next to a truck. A second ago he tapped Clock in, or Clock out.
**The one decision or action this exists for:** Get the driver through the legally required check as fast as possible with no guessing, never trapped, never confused about what is missing.
**SMART HINTS (the core requirement, apply everywhere):**
- A primary button is never silently disabled. If something is missing, the button stays tappable-looking but a hint line directly above it says exactly what is missing in plain words ("Add your ending odometer to save"), and tapping it scrolls to and highlights the missing field.
- A hint bar at the top of each form summarises what is left: "2 checks left" / "Odometer needed" / "All done — ready to send", each item tappable to jump to it.
- Every field shows what it expects before the driver types: start reading, last reading, typical range ("Start: 72,276 km. Usually 5–300 km more."), numeric keypad, units. Hours default from the clock times so nothing is typed that the phone already knows; the driver can change them.
- Problems are explained before they are sent: ending odometer below start ("72,270 is lower than the start of 72,276 — check it") or very high ("That is 1,800 km — more than the 1,500 km we expect. Tick 'Yes, that is right' if so"); the confirm control only appears when needed.
- Errors name the cause and the next step with a button ("The office did not accept this because …  [Fix and send again]  [Call the office]"). No internal words anywhere (queue, ledger, marker, server status, report type, declaration).
- Offline / waiting states are honest and calm: "Saved on this phone. It will send when you have signal. You can keep working." with a small sent/waiting indicator, never a blocking error.
- Always a way out: every screen has Back or "Not now" (what happens next is stated: "You will stay clocked in"), and "Call the office" is one tap away on any error or DO NOT DRIVE.
- Each required question has a one-line "Why we ask": e.g. "Required by the commercial vehicle log."
**States to show:** (1) driving dialog: Yes / No, first launch, after "No"; (2) vehicle check: empty, in progress with hint bar, one failed non-critical item, a failed CRITICAL item (DO NOT DRIVE, Call the office, queue report), all passed, saved-offline, sent; (3) finish trip: odometer missing, odometer too low, odometer too high with confirm, ready, saving offline, saved, refused by the office with Fix-and-send-again, success with "You are clocked out"; also a "Not now" path.
**Synthetic content:** vehicle "Truck 1 (RAM 3500)", start odometer 72,276, driver "Alex Example", 12 checklist items in groups (Lights, Tyres and wheels, Brakes, Fluids, Safety kit) such as "Headlights work", "Tyres look inflated", "Brakes feel firm", "No fluid leaks", "Fire extinguisher present", office phone (555) 010-0100.
**Files wanted in out/:** `trip-flow.html` (all screens and states, switchable via data-theme / data-mode; the three moments as a labelled storyboard), `states.html` (every state of each moment side by side), `NOTES.md` (design decisions, the hint rules as a table: situation -> hint text -> action, and anything you could not satisfy).
**Must reuse:** tokens in `tokens.css` only; Inter; status = icon + word; 1.75 px icons; the crew phone patterns in the v1.2 spec (48 dp targets, 56 dp main action, docked bottom action bar inside its own area — nothing floats over content).
**Must NOT do:** colour as the only signal; a disabled button without an adjacent reason; more than one filled primary button per screen; a "Report type" selector; the words queue / ledger / marker / server status / declaration in visible text (say "driving question" or "your answer" where needed); text under 12 px; hex literals; a modal that cannot be left.
**Hard requirements:** WCAG 2.2 AAA (7:1 text, 4.5:1 focus ring, 48 dp targets, 56 dp main action); text scales to 200% without clipping; works at 375, 768, 1024, 1440 in light, dark and field mode; field mode legible in direct sun.
