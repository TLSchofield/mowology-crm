When a job lands, what should it do?

Six ways to show that a job moved. Each box has the old and the new job beside it. Press the button on a box, or "Play all".

With "reduce motion" on in your system, B to E do not move at all and F shows a plain highlight. In the real page the ring or edge sits on the job that moved, and the toast "Rescheduled visit" appears at the same time.
