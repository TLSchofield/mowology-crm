# Creative Director critique rubric
Look at the PNGs (Read them) at 375 first, then 1440, light then field. Write each point as `+ strong (keep)` or `- problem (fix)` and turn the `-` lines into the next brief.

1. **Next action in one second?** Squint: can you find the dominant action and the urgent items from across the room? More than one filled button per decision = fail.
2. **Hierarchy** — type sizes and weights say what matters; whitespace is even (no big gap beside tight padding); one accent, not three competing brand colours.
3. **Status** — every status is icon + word and uses the right vocabulary; none depends on colour.
4. **Information scent** — each item states what is at stake (money, days waiting), not just a label.
5. **Density and rhythm** — rows before cards; consistent row height; no card-in-card.
6. **Outdoors** — field mode legible in sun; targets reachable with gloves; main action in the thumb zone.
7. **Honest states** — pending/offline/failed are designed, not implied.
8. **Consistency** — same icon style (1.75 px stroke), no emoji mixed with line icons, no native select in prime space.
9. **System fit** — would a designer who knew v1.2 think this belongs? New patterns are proposed in NOTES.md, not smuggled in.
10. **Machine checks** — `checks.md` clean at all 12 combinations, or the remaining failures are explained (e.g. token-level AAA gap in SKILL.md).

Lessons from earlier Ittai rounds (2026-10-16/19): 12 equal filled "Open" buttons made nothing obvious; three surface colours (green block, slate sidebar, lime active) fought; an emoji next to line icons looked broken; a red "Clock In" label read as an error; hexagon labels at 8 px were unreadable on a phone; the first "today" mock shipped desktop-only.
